# Layout audit, 2026-10 (before the unification pass)

Method: every screen and state was rendered offscreen by `Lasero.Tests/LayoutAuditRenderTests.cs` (real `MainWindow`, real
view models, the built-in simulator, a scratch profile folder, no input of any kind) at 1366x768, 1080x640 and 1920x1080,
plus the windows and overlays at their native sizes. Run it with `LASERO_AUDIT=1 LASERO_RENDER_OUT=<dir>`; without `LASERO_AUDIT` the audit
tests return at once. The PNGs are not committed (about 8 MB per set): before renders are in `E:/lasero-layout-renders/before`,
after renders in `E:/lasero-layout-renders/after` on the author machine, and are regenerated by the command above.

Legend for defects: **A** alignment or edge, **G** off the 4 px grid, **R** radius, **H** height, **P** padding, **T** type
size or weight, **I** icon size or stroke, **C** crowded or clipped, **D** dead space, **X** competing primary, **N** unclear
hierarchy, **E** empty state, **S** shared pattern missing (same job, different markup), **B** dialog button order.

Cross-cutting findings (each is repeated in the table of every screen it touches):

1. **No shared screen header.** Home has an avatar greeting, Zařízení a 34 px title with a subtitle and two controls, Chat has
   no title at all, Settings and Materiály put a 32 px icon in front of the title and indent the subtitle by 32, Nastavení
   zařízení / Připravit obrázek / Offset křivky / Klávesové zkratky each use another title size and padding, Login a
   fourth. Left page margin: 16 (Zařízení), 24 (Home), 36 (Settings, Materiály), 32 (shortcuts), 24 (raster, offset).
2. **Spacing is not on a grid.** About 800 literal `Margin` and `Padding` values, 38 distinct off-grid ones (3, 5, 6, 7, 9,
   10, 14, 18, 22 and half pixel pairs such as `9,4`). 10 and 14 are the most common values in the product.
3. **Three card paddings and two header styles inside the same screen.** Zařízení: "Stav stroje" card title is 17 px,
   "Nastavení gravírky" card title is 13 px bold, "Ruční připojení" has an icon in front of its title, the others do not.
4. **Empty states are boxes in some places** (Zařízení "Nejprve připojte zařízení" is a filled box with an icon, Chat history
   has an icon plus a bold 13 px title, Materiály detail is a card) **and one line in others** (Home).
5. **Tip dne was shown twice on Home** (card plus a leftover line). Fixed in the first commit of this pass.
6. **Dialogs have one anatomy in `LaseroDialogWindow` and four other ones** in Settings, Device settings, Raster import and
   Offset: different footer heights (66, 72, 56), different cancel treatments (outline, ghost with X icon, secondary), the
   Offset window has no title bar at all.
7. **The right panel has no single pattern**: Home device rail (300 px, header 17 px), Návrh inspector (360 px, headers 17 px
   plus a 13 px device line with a different icon size), Zařízení right column (2/5 of the content, no fixed width).
8. **Hint area for "what next" does not exist.** The status strip message is the only candidate and it is truncated to a
   few characters at 1080 px.
