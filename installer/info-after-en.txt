Setup is complete - a few notes for testing

Test version
LASERO Desktop is in closed testing. Content, look and behaviour may change between versions.

Safety
- before the first real job, rehearse on a scrap piece of material while wearing laser safety glasses
- compatibility with specific machine models is not yet validated; connecting to a machine is at the operator's own risk and under supervision
- recommended speed and power values are not validated for a particular machine and material combination; always confirm them on a sample
- never leave a running job unattended

Reporting problems
Send a description, the steps that led to it, the machine model and firmware to the person who gave you this installer, or via https://lasero.net. Files from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lasero\logs help too. They contain the application's operating log; you can review them before sending.

USB driver
If the engraver does not show up as a COM port after connecting the cable, the USB serial driver may be missing. The next page has a short overview.
